# Trust Evidence Positioning Design

## Decision

Do not add a standalone trust strip after the hero. The light trust band interrupts the full dark hero composition, repeats existing positioning, and gives four supporting facts too much visual weight.

## Hero

Keep the hero as one uninterrupted composition. Preserve its existing compact facts row:

- `Kesetaraan SMA`
- `Programmer & Designer`
- `Kota Semarang`

These facts support the headline without creating a second visual section between the hero and the NUSA tagline.

## Industry Partners

Keep `PartnerSection` as a separate section later on the landing page. The company logos demonstrate that the technology program is informed by a real professional network.

Partner copy must remain neutral. Do not imply that partner companies guarantee internships, recruitment, job placement, or paid projects for students.

## Student Opportunities

Explain the actual opportunity model in the program or outcome content:

- Students build practical work and portfolios during their education.
- Portfolios become preparation for independently seeking internships, freelance projects, or employment.
- The school does not promise placement or guaranteed income.

The existing benefits section already states that income opportunities and outcomes can vary. Preserve that clarification.

## Scope

No production UI change is required because the rejected trust-strip implementation was isolated on an unmerged feature branch. `main` already retains the approved hero facts row and partner section.

## Out Of Scope

- Adding a trust strip after the hero.
- Removing the partner section.
- Claiming formal recruitment or internship placement.
- Adding unverifiable student, partner, or outcome statistics.
